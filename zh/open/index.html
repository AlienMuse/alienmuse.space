英文原文

Thirty-five come from the two works on the architecture of a reasoning system and twenty-two from the five works that each take a named conjecture or programme as research subject; no problem is one of those conjectures.

02每部著作留下的开放问题#

七部著作都不声称证明了它所研究的问题。

Series 00 / §1.5

英文原文

None of the seven works claims a proof of the problems it studies.

  1. Series 00

    The Grothendieckian Programme

    本书是这七部著作的总纲，不在七部之外增加任何数学结果。

    该书的边界，用它自己的话 / 摘要

    英文原文

    This book is their overview, and it adds no mathematical result to them.

  2. Series 01

    GAI-MUA: The Grothendieckian Machine

    “Grothendieckian Machine”这一名称指一个研究架构，而不是一个已完成的通用求解器。

    该书的边界，用它自己的话 / Series Note

    英文原文

    The phrase Grothendieckian Machine names a research architecture, not a completed universal solver.

  3. Series 02

    GM-Omega-Infinity: Topos-Native Intelligence

    本书“已证明若干局部特化，而没有已证明的整体包含结果或替代结果”。

    该书的边界，用它自己的话 / 摘要

    英文原文

    has several proved local specialisations and no proved global subsumption or replacement result.

  4. Series 03

    Arithmetic Spectral Descent

    黎曼猜想仍未解决。本书不声称证明了它，也不声称证明了广义黎曼猜想或任何等价命题。

    该书的边界，用它自己的话 / Series Note

    英文原文

    The Riemann Hypothesis remains open. This work does not claim a proof of it, of the generalized Riemann hypotheses, or of any equivalent statement.

  5. Series 04

    Fractured Adelic Geometry

    Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer 猜想仍未解决。本书没有证明它，没有证明任何此前未知的情形，也没有对任何一条曲线证明 Ш（Tate–Shafarevich 群）的有限性。

    该书的边界，用它自己的话 / Series Note

    英文原文

    The Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture remains open. This work does not prove it, proves no case of it that was not already known, and proves the finiteness of Ш for no curve.

  6. Series 05

    The Archimedean Prism

    Hodge 猜想仍未解决。本书没有证明它，也没有证明任何此前未知的情形。

    该书的边界，用它自己的话 / Series Note

    英文原文

    The Hodge conjecture remains open. This work does not prove it, and proves no case of it that was not already known.

  7. Series 06

    Strained Prismatic Geometry

    abc 猜想仍未解决。本书没有证明它。

    该书的边界，用它自己的话 / Series Note

    英文原文

    The abc conjecture remains open. This work does not prove it.

  8. Series 07

    The Arithmetic Topos

    本书没有证明 Langlands 对应，书中也没有无条件地确立其主猜想的任何一种情形。

    该书的边界，用它自己的话 / Series Note

    英文原文

    This work does not prove the Langlands correspondence, and no case of its Main Conjecture is established unconditionally in it.
